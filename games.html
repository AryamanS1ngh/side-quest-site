New to Tabletop Games?

Come to Learn-a-Game Night on the first Wednesday of each month, 7:00 to 8:30 PM. Someone from our staff picks a game and teaches it.

Or join Open Table Thursday, every Thursday from 6:30 to 9:30 PM. Bring your own game or grab one from our shelves. Coming alone is completely fine.
